# The programme

Thirty days, five chapters. Each chapter teaches one technique and ends with a test. Worked back from memoryOS's course and the standard memory-sport curriculum (association, visualisation, linking, loci, names, numbers).

Every day has the same shape so it never needs explaining: Priya explains (animated scenes), you practise (a game built for that technique), you're tested, you get a score. 3 to 5 minutes.

## Chapter 1: Picture it (Days 1 to 6)
The brain remembers pictures, not words. Make them big, silly, moving.

| Day | Lesson | Game |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story Chain: link items into a silly story | Tap in order, questions, before and after test |
| 2 | Picture it: boring vs vivid, the 3 rules (big, silly, moving) | Pairs: see a pair, make a picture, recall the partner |
| 3 | Association: hook a new thing to something you already know | Hooks: match new words to home objects |
| 4 | Story Chain, 10 items | Longer chain, timed |
| 5 | Where did I keep it: the "say it out loud" anchor | Find-it game: rooms and objects |
| 6 | Chapter test | All three games, short |

## Chapter 2: Memory Palace (Days 7 to 12)
Your own home is the filing cabinet.

| Day | Lesson | Game |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Your first palace: 5 spots in your home, always in the same order | Walk: tap the rooms in order |
| 8 | Put a list in the palace | Place: put each item at a spot, then walk and recall |
| 9 | Walk it backwards | Reverse walk |
| 10 | Second room: 10 spots | Place 10 |
| 11 | A to-do list in the palace | Place, then recall next morning (review) |
| 12 | Chapter test | Place and walk, 10 items |

## Chapter 3: Names and faces (Days 13 to 18)
Face feature, name sound, silly picture.

| Day | Lesson | Game |

| 13 | Why names slip: you never heard it | Listen and repeat |
| 14 | Name to picture: Sharma becomes a sharmila (shy) face | Name pictures |
| 15 | Feature hook: the big nose wears the picture | Faces (illustrated) with names |
| 16 | Relatives and neighbours | Family tree game |
| 17 | New people at a function | Mixed faces |
| 18 | Chapter test | 8 faces |

## Chapter 4: Numbers (Days 19 to 24)
Each digit is a shape: 1 is a candle, 2 a swan, 3 a heart on its side...

| Day | Lesson | Game |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | Number shapes 0 to 9 | Match digit to shape |
| 20 | Two digits make a scene | Scenes |
| 21 | Phone numbers | 10 digits in a story |
| 22 | PINs and dates | Birthday game |
| 23 | Numbers in the palace | Place digits |
| 24 | Chapter test | A phone number and a date |

## Chapter 5: Long text (Days 25 to 30)
Shlokas, speeches, recipes: key words in the palace.

| Day | Lesson | Game |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | Key words: a verse is 5 pictures | Pick the key words |
| 26 | A shloka in the palace | Place and recite |
| 27 | A recipe | Ingredient chain |
| 28 | A short speech | 5 points in 5 rooms |
| 29 | Review everything | Mixed |
| 30 | Final test and certificate | All techniques |

## Scoring
Every day is out of 100: 10 for finishing, the rest from the games. Brain score is a rolling average of the last 5 days, so one bad day doesn't crash it. Chapter badges unlock on the chapter test.

## Review
Lists and palaces come back 1, 3 and 7 days later as a one-minute review in the chat. Reviews earn points too.

## Built so far
Days 1, 2 and 7 are built as the proof of the engine. Each new day is a content file, no new code, unless it needs a new game.
