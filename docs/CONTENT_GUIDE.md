# Content guide

How every lesson and quiz is written. Keep to this so Day 30 sounds like Day 1.

## Voice

A friendly coach. Warm, encouraging, never talks down. Uses "aap". Think of a kind family doctor who is pleased to see you.

## Language

Simple English with Hindi words where they feel natural (doodh, sabzi, shabash, yaad). Short sentences. No jargon. If a word would confuse someone with basic English, use the Hindi word or a simpler English one.

## Shape of a day

1. Lesson, 3 screens, each under 40 words
2. Practice, one list or set to memorise using today's technique
3. Quiz, 5 questions, 4 options each, big buttons
4. Score screen with one line of praise and one thing to try at home

Under 5 minutes total.

## Rules

- One idea per screen
- Wrong answers get a gentle "not this one" plus the reason, never "wrong"
- Every quiz tests the technique, not trivia
- Examples come from daily life in an Indian home: sabzi lists, phone numbers, festival dates, where the keys are
- Brain score: 40 for finishing, 12 per correct answer, so a perfect day is 100

## Where content lives

Each day's text is in the `DAY1` block at the top of the script in `app/index.html`. Edit the words there, nothing else.
